Recovery point

Is every database covered?

Newest full and log backup at the same capture, from msdb. This is coverage, not an RPO claim: the log age is observed, and a three-hour gap on 24 November is recorded on the backup page. Before the audit this table listed only the original databases; the three governance databases were absent from it and had no backup history. The hourly health check did include them and stayed CRITICAL until full backups existed.

Health checks

What a DBA looks at first

Availability

All nine databases ONLINE, no suspect pages, no RECOVERY_PENDING state.

Growth

File growth increments are fixed-size, not percentage: a percentage growth on a large file stalls writes while it expands.

Backup age

A missing log backup turns a 15-minute target into an unknown recovery point; the watcher exists for exactly that. It caught the three governance databases with no full backup, and returned to OK after the gap was closed (before · after).
