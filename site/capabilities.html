The problem

Why this exists, in four numbers that are not mine

Database work is judged by what happens on the worst day. These published figures describe the size of that day, and the rules that now surround it.

Published factNumberSource
Share of enterprises where one hour of unplanned downtime costs $300,000 or more93%ITIC, 2025 reliability survey, 2,000 respondents
Enterprises whose hourly downtime exceeds $1 million46%ITIC, 2025
Respondents whose most recent major outage cost over $100,00057%Uptime Institute 2025 survey, annual outage analysis 2026
Date EU financial entities had to document and test backup and restoration procedures (DORA)17 Jan 2025Regulation (EU) 2022/2554

The same obligation exists in other jurisdictions, with local deadlines — Nigeria's CBN data-localisation circular reaches full compliance on 1 January 2027 and covers database administration, backup and recovery explicitly. The mechanism is identical everywhere: a firm must be able to say what it would lose, and prove it can get it back. That is the work this lab practises.

Limits

What this is not

Not production software, never touched real money, not certified against any regulation, and not a demonstration of oil-and-gas domain knowledge. It is a lab built to the standard those industries demand — provenance, measured recovery, and a written record of what failed — with the gaps listed above rather than hidden. What is claimed was measured, and the output is on the evidence page.
